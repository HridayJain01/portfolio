Nobody reads your landing page. They watch the first eight seconds of your demo and decide. So the demo has to work fast.

## Script the story, not the features

A good demo has one hero moment. Find the single interaction that makes people say *"oh, nice"* and build everything around reaching it as fast as possible.

1. **Hook (0–3s)**: show the result first.
2. **How (3–20s)**: the two or three steps that get you there.
3. **Payoff (20–30s)**: the result again, now earned.

## Record like a pro

- Use a clean demo account with believable data.
- Bump UI scale so text is readable on a phone.
- Hide the cursor or replace it with a smooth, enlarged one.
- Export at 1080p+, then compress hard for the web.

## Keep it light

A looping, muted, 10-second clip will be watched far more than a two-minute walkthrough. Put the long version one click away for the people who want it.
